College Performance \& Fees Analytics



Project Overview



College Performance \& Fees Analytics is a SQL-based data analysis project developed using MySQL.



The project analyzes fictional college data to understand student enrollment, fee collection, pending payments and academic performance.



Project Objectives



\- Analyze course-wise student enrollment.

\- Track fee collection and pending fees.

\- Identify students with unpaid or partially paid fees.

\- Evaluate course-wise and subject-wise academic performance.

\- Identify failed students and top performers.

\- Rank courses based on fee collection.



Database Information



\- Database Name: college\_analytics

\- Database Management System: MySQL

\- SQL Tool: MySQL Workbench

\- Tables: 6

\- Business Questions: 12



Database Tables



1\. students – Student details such as name, city, age and joining date.

2\. courses – Course information.

3\. enrollment – Student enrollment and enrollment status.

4\. fees – Fee payment details.

5\. subjects – Subject information.

6\. results – Student marks and result status.



Business Questions



1\. How many students are enrolled in each course?

2\. How much fee has been collected for each course?

3\. How much fee is pending for each course?

4\. Which students have unpaid or partially paid fees?

5\. What is the average age of students in each city?

6\. How many enrollments are there for each enrollment status?

7\. Which courses have an average score above 60?

8\. What is the average score in each subject?

9\. Which students have failed, and in which subjects?

10\. What is the pass percentage for each course?

11\. Who are the top-performing students?

12\. How do courses rank by total fee collection?



Key Insights



\- BCA and MCA have the highest enrollment, with 4 students each.

\- MBA has the highest fee collection of ₹1,80,000.

\- MCA has the highest pending fees of ₹1,50,000.

\- Latur has the highest average student age of approximately 22.67 years.

\- MBA has the highest average marks among courses with an average above 60.

\- Financial Management has the highest subject-wise average score of 69.33.

\- BCA and MBA have the highest pass percentage of 100%.

\- Aditya Kulkarni is the top-performing student with an average score of 91.

\- MBA ranks first in total fee collection.
